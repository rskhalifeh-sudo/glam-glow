About Us

At Glam & Glow in Kaslik, we believe everyone deserves to feel confident in their own skin. Founded by Grace, a Biology graduate from the Lebanese University Hadath, Glam & Glow is built on scientific knowledge, continuous education, and a passion for helping clients achieve healthy and radiant skin. Every treatment is tailored to the individual, ensuring a comfortable one-on-one experience focused entirely on your needs. From Medical Skincare and Microneedling to Lash Lifting and Slimming Treatments, our services are designed to deliver safe, effective, and lasting results. Our mission is simple: to make professional skincare accessible, luxurious, and personalized, so every client leaves feeling confident, refreshed, glowing and alive.
